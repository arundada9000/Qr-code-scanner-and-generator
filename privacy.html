Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 July 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how SajiloQR ("we", "the site") handles information when you use the QR code generator and scanner at easyqrcreate.netlify.app. SajiloQR is a free tool built and maintained by Arun Neupane. The short version: the tool runs almost entirely in your own browser, and we do not collect or store your personal data on any server.

Information handled in your browser

  • QR content: The links, text, and Wi-Fi details you enter to generate a code are processed locally in your browser to draw the QR image. They are never uploaded to us.
  • Scanning and camera: When you use the scanner, your camera feed is read directly in the browser to decode a code. The video is never recorded, stored, or transmitted. Camera access is requested only when you press "Start Camera".
  • Local history: Your recent generated and scanned items are saved in your browser's local storage so the history list persists between visits. This data stays on your device, and the "Clear All" button removes it instantly.

Cookies and local storage

SajiloQR itself does not set tracking cookies. It uses your browser's local storage only for the convenience feature described above. However, third parties described below may set cookies.

Advertising and Google AdSense

This site may display ads served by Google, as a third-party vendor, using the Google AdSense program. In connection with serving ads:

  • Google and its partners may use cookies (including the DoubleClick cookie) to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to this site and/or other sites on the internet.
  • You may opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings. You can also opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalised advertising at aboutads.info.
  • For more information on how Google uses data when you use our partners' sites, see Google's Privacy & Terms.

Third-party libraries

To keep the tool lightweight, some resources (web fonts, icons, and the QR generation and scanning libraries) load from public content delivery networks such as Google Fonts, Cloudflare, and jsDelivr. When your browser requests these files, the provider may receive your IP address as part of a standard web request. We do not control these providers' policies.

Children's privacy

SajiloQR is a general-audience utility and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the tool evolves or as advertising requirements change. The "last updated" date at the top reflects the latest revision.

Contact

If you have any questions about this policy or how the tool works, please use the contact page. We read every message.
